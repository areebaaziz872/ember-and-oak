The philosophy

Built around
the fire.

At Ember & Oak, the hearth is more than a cooking method. It shapes the pace, flavor, and character of the evening.

Imagined as a single room organised around an open fire, the concept follows the seasons: a short menu, a slow rhythm, and plates finished over oak and embers. Nothing is rushed. The glow sets the tempo, and the table follows.

    Fire / Craft

    Fire changes
    everything.

    Live fire is unforgiving and generous in equal measure. Oak builds the heat, embers hold it steady, and each ingredient is placed where it needs to be — close enough to take on colour and smoke, far enough to stay tender. It is a slower way to cook, and the plate shows it.

    Private dining
